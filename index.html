Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters.

Can I keep stock in more than one warehouse?

Yes. Stock is listed by warehouse.

Can I record stock movements?

Yes. There are areas for movements, amendments and bulk adjustments.

Does it handle price lists and bundles?

Yes. Price lists, bundle items and packages are all supported.

Is my data kept per company?

Yes. Each company has its own separate data, and everyone in that company sees the same records.
